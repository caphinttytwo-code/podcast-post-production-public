# Branch: casual-non-rigorous

## Branch Label

- ID: `casual-non-rigorous`
- Chinese label: `轻松且非严谨内容`
- Status: active; shared ChatCut/agent-reference memory gate added in `v1.4.0`; StepFun API base standardized in `v1.4.1`

Use this branch for relaxed solo commentary, personal observations, entertainment, informal creator talk, or low-stakes knowledge sharing. Do not use it for news, research, documentary claims, legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, or exact instructional content.

Read [editorial-decision-rules.md](editorial-decision-rules.md) first. Apply this file only for branch-specific content tolerance and pacing.

## Editorial Character

Make the show feel intentionally shorter, not mechanically scrubbed. Follow the earlier edit's macro-content instinct—remove whole low-value passages—while retaining the newer master's clean sound and basic surface cleanup.

### Remove first

1. Recording debris: starts, countdowns, pauses, water breaks, livestream housekeeping, and abandoned takes.
2. Repeated explanations that restate the same conclusion without a new example, joke, or emotional beat.
3. Tool, software, equipment, or process tangents that do not pay off in the episode's central promise.
4. Overlong enumerations where one or two examples already establish the point.
5. Repeated transitions, self-summary, and setup that the next passage immediately repeats.
6. False starts, immediate stutters, context-verified standalone fillers, coughs between clauses, and abnormal work silence.

### Keep

- The episode identity and a clean opening greeting.
- The core claim of each section and enough context for a cold listener.
- Personal quirks, playful phrasing, punchlines, and brief rhetorical repetition that create personality.
- Examples that prove or humanize a claim rather than merely adding another item.
- Necessary transitions, callbacks, conclusions, and the complete closing.
- Uncertain material when deletion could change meaning.

## Macro-Cut Gate

Delete a complete passage only when all answers are safe:

1. Does the next retained sentence remain understandable without it?
2. Does the passage add no unique claim, evidence, joke, emotional turn, or necessary setup?
3. Does no later sentence refer back to it?
4. Can its purpose be described as repetition, digression, over-detail, or recording debris rather than disagreement with the speaker?

If any answer is unclear, keep the passage or create a review candidate instead of a final cut.

## Pacing Defaults

- Treat 3–8% duration reduction as a sanity range, never as a quota.
- Prefer one 10–30 second macro cut over many adjacent word cuts when both preserve the same meaning.
- Use pause duration only to find candidates. Shorten ordinary outlier pauses toward the episode's natural 0.6–0.8 second range when meaning and delivery allow; never force every pause into that range.
- Use micro-cuts for obvious stutters and debris, not to rewrite spontaneous speech into formal prose.
- Preserve natural breaths, companionship, laughter, comic timing, emotional hesitation, and space around section changes.
- Read [pause-shaping.md](pause-shaping.md) and preserve its role hierarchy; do not import the rigorous branch's tighter explanatory rhythm.

## Review Checklist

- Play the sentence before, the cut, and the sentence after as one unit.
- Confirm that pronouns, comparisons, numbers, and callbacks still have referents.
- Compare retained transcript coverage, not only total duration.
- Flag deleted examples or explanatory blocks explicitly in edit notes.
- Reject a shorter version when it removes useful information but retains obvious stutters or recording debris.
- Re-transcribe the rendered candidate and pass sentence, logic, pacing, and persona checks before release.

## Branch Boundary

When an episode needs factual completeness or source-traceable claims, route it to `rigorous-explainer.md`. Do not weaken this boundary by adding exceptions here.
